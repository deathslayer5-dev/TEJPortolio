Unit 3 - Circuits

Main Idea

This unit revolves around circuits and hardware rather than computers, it relies on logic gates and breadboard circuitry

Activities

The first hands-on activity we had on circuitry. At the start of this unit, I did not understand the importance of having neat wires, neglecting to cut them to the minimal length, leading to a messy feel to my first design.

In this activity, I was tasked with making a circuit that would showcase my learning on the unit so far, the circuit was specified to be a AND gate. The circuit I had made was an obvious improvement to what I had made in my intro activity, showcasing clear wiring and minimal distances when it came to wires, allowing my circuit to see more minimal and clear.

This was a similar activity than the previous, comparing to the AND gate, the OR gate was not that far off, both requiring 2 inputs and would be true when both inputs are true. The key difference in this activity came from the truth table, while the OR gate would be true when either inputs are on, the AND gate would require both inputs to be on for the output to be true.

While both the OR gate and the AND gate required 2 inputs, the NOT gate would only need 1, as it is just an inverter than turns 0 into 1 and 1 into 0, making the truth table a lot simpler.

A NOR gate is a logic gate that differs from the rest that already has been mentioned, composing of a OR gate and a NOT gate. It takes the output of a normal OR gate and inverts it.

A NAND gate is a logic gate that is like the NOR gate, same in the way that it used two components which are the NOT gate and the AND gate. It takes the output of a normal AND gate and inverts it.

This is my own custom gate, it also represents an emulated OR gate. This connects an NOT gate into AND gate into another NOT gate. This gate shows my understanding in wiring and logic gates.

This is an activity that revolves around having 3 traffic lights that has 3 buttons, with different resistors. This shows that different amount of resistors, would make different light levels.

Reflection

This unit was rather hands-on and gave most students the experience of wiring and electrical components, this would be essential to understand further on future units.

Unit 4 - Micro:bit

Main Idea

This unit revolves around the micro:bit component, using it to construct different ideas and projects!

Activities

This was my intro to Micro:bit a small component that acts as a brain, with a 5x5 grid of LEDs to show a variety of patterns. I was tasked to make the micro:bit display my name as well as a fun fact, this was meant to loop infinitely until the micro:bit would run out of battery.

This was an activity which tested my coding abilities, I was tasked with making two pictures of a heart, displaying them with the 5x5 LED grid. I was told to mimic the delays of heartbeats, making it seem as if it was a real heart. I used a forever loop with two images of the heart, so it would seem as if it contracts for a short moment and expands after, giving a short pulse.

This assignment was making an emoji that you had to make to flash many times, mimicking the emoji fading. This assignment tested my ability to draw as well as my logical thinking skills, when it came to making the emoji seem like it is disappearing.

This assignment was for everytime you moved, the step counter would increase, based on your stride length. This assignment tested my micro:bit's ability to sense movement and tested its functionality overall.

This assignment test tested on the micro:bit's light sensing, using it to power on the inbuilt LEDs This assignment was extremely helpful for students who were still questioning the functions of the micro:bit.

This assignment tasked me to build a program which would cycle between rock, paper and scissors randomly, following the famous game of rock paper scissors. This assignment showed me how to use random numbers in micro:bit as well as how to use the in-built radio to play with others.

This was the end of unit project, I was tasked with making a micro:bit program attached to a kind of hardware and present it as a business pitch. This project trained my presentation skills, hardware building skills, wiring skills and more. This project was definitely one of a kind and helped me understand more about the use cases of the micro:bit.

Reflection

The Micro:bit project was probably the most eye-opening unit to the future of people who want to pursue this course, the skills needed were clearly demonstrated in this project, a brief "introduction" to those who want to be an engineer in this career.
