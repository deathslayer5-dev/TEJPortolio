Hardware

Main Idea

This unit revolves around the different hardware components in a computer, and eventually building a computer!

Activities

Me, Branko and George worked on a user manual designed to teach people how to disassemble and reassemble a computer. This assignment required us to record a video of us disassembling a computer with another video reassembling the computer, also including before and after pictures

In this activity, I had to go research components for a computer that had to fit a certain budget, a $900 computer and a $8000 computer. For this task, I used the website PC part picker to find my components, checking compatibility and searching for parts under my budget. Through this task, I learnt a lot about computer parts and the market for them, allowing me to start preparing for building my own.

Reflection

Overall, this unit was especially fun, not only did it allow hands-on experiences, but also allowed us to plan a built computer ourselves. With a set budget, it allows us to think of our decisions more clearly and allows us to think more realistically.

Circuits

Main Idea

This unit revolves around circuits and hardware rather than computers, it relies on logic gates and breadboard circuitry

Activities

The first hands-on activity we had on circuitry. At the start of this unit, I did not understand the importance of having neat wires, neglecting to cut them to the minimal length, leading to a messy feel to my first design.

In this activity, I was tasked with making a circuit that would showcase my learning on the unit so far, the circuit was specified to be a AND gate. The circuit I had made was an obvious improvement to what I had made in my intro activity, showcasing clear wiring and minimal distances when it came to wires, allowing my circuit to see more minimal and clear.

This was a similar activity than the previous, comparing to the AND gate, the OR gate was not that far off, both requiring 2 inputs and would be true when both inputs are true. The key difference in this activity came from the truth table, while the OR gate would be true when either inputs are on, the AND gate would require both inputs to be on for the output to be true.

While both the OR gate and the AND gate required 2 inputs, the NOT gate would only need 1, as it is just an inverter than turns 0 into 1 and 1 into 0, making the truth table a lot simpler.

A NOR gate is a logic gate that differs from the rest that already has been mentioned, composing of a OR gate and a NOT gate. It takes the output of a normal OR gate and inverts it.

A NAND gate is a logic gate that is like the NOR gate, same in the way that it used two components which are the NOT gate and the AND gate. It takes the output of a normal AND gate and inverts it.

This is my own custom gate, it also represents an emulated OR gate. This connects an NOT gate into AND gate into another NOT gate. This gate shows my understanding in wiring and logic gates.

This is an activity that revolves around having 3 traffic lights that has 3 buttons, with different resistors. This shows that different amount of resistors, would make different light levels.

Reflection

This unit was rather hands-on and gave most students the experience of wiring and electrical components, this would be essential to understand further on future units.
